## AI and voice

In Apple Notes you can record audio, get a transcript and, on devices with Apple Intelligence, a summary of the recording. Calls recorded on an iPhone land in Notes too. Apple Intelligence Writing Tools can rewrite or shorten selected text. What Notes lacks is a chat that answers questions from all your notes.

FixNote's [assistant](/features/ask-your-notes/) searches by words and by meaning in three languages, answers with links and edits notes, showing each change as a diff. You choose the model: FixNote AI with Pro, your own key, or Ollama. [Voice notes](/features/voice-notes/) are transcribed on the device, and [call summaries](/features/call-summaries/) on Windows and macOS 14.2+ are built from the microphone and the computer's audio, so they work with Zoom, Teams or any other call app.

## Encryption

By default iCloud encrypts notes in transit and on its servers, and Apple holds the keys. Notes become end-to-end encrypted once you turn on Advanced Data Protection yourself. Locked notes are encrypted with their password either way.

FixNote's [end-to-end encryption](/features/encryption/) covers every note, folder and image from the start. The keys come from a 12-word phrase, which the app shows only after a code sent to your email.

## Platforms and price

Apple Notes runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and other computers reach it through iCloud.com. It is free, and sync goes through iCloud.

FixNote runs on Windows, macOS and in the browser, phones included. If you have an iPhone and a Windows PC, Pro syncs your notes between them, and on the iPhone FixNote opens in Safari. Free never expires; Pro is $7 a month or $60 a year and adds sync, FixNote AI, shared notes and the Telegram bot.

## How to move your notes from Apple Notes

1. Install FixNote on your Mac and open Settings → Data → Import notes.
2. Click Apple Notes. macOS asks whether FixNote may use Notes; allow it.
3. Choose the folders to bring over. Nothing changes in Apple Notes.

Locked notes are skipped, and FixNote tells you how many there were. PDFs, scans and drawings don't come over, and FixNote counts those too. If you once denied access, turn on Notes for FixNote in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation. More on the [import and export page](/features/import-export/).
