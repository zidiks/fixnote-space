## AI and search

Evernote has added a lot of AI in recent years: AI Assistant in a chat, semantic search, AI Transcribe for recording and transcribing meetings, and AI editing. Its plan comparison ticks these features on every plan, but on Free you work with 50 notes at most. Connecting your notes to Claude and ChatGPT over MCP starts with Starter.

FixNote's [assistant](/features/ask-your-notes/) searches by meaning and by words, answers with links, and creates and edits notes. Every change shows up as a diff and can be undone in Settings → AI. You choose the model: FixNote AI with Pro, your own key, or Ollama on your computer. FixNote writes [call summaries](/features/call-summaries/) on Windows and macOS 14.2+: speech is transcribed on the device during the call and no audio is kept. The [MCP server](/features/mcp/) is free and runs on your computer.

## Privacy

Evernote encrypts data on its servers, and paid plans let you lock selected text in a note with a passphrase in the Mac and Windows apps. The service can read everything else.

FixNote's [end-to-end encryption](/features/encryption/) covers every note, folder name and image. The keys come from a 12-word phrase that is never on the server. If you don't need sync, Only on this device mode stops the app from contacting our server at all.

## Price and platforms

Evernote Free gives you 50 notes, one notebook and one device. Starter is $14.99 a month or $99 a year; Advanced is $24.99 a month or $249.99 a year. There are apps for Windows, macOS, the web, iPhone and Android.

FixNote Free costs nothing, never expires and has no note limit. Pro is $7 a month or $60 a year and includes sync, FixNote AI, shared notes and the [Telegram bot](/features/telegram/). FixNote runs on Windows, macOS and in the browser, and on phones in the browser too.

## How to move your notes from Evernote

FixNote doesn't read `.enex` files, so there is one extra step.

1. In Evernote on your computer, open the notebook list and export the notebook you want as ENEX.
2. Install the free Obsidian app, turn on its official Importer plugin and import the `.enex` file. You get a folder of Markdown notes with their images.
3. In FixNote, open Settings → Data → Import notes, click Choose folder and pick that folder.

Images become attachments; PDFs and other files are skipped and counted. More on the [import and export page](/features/import-export/).
